## v1.2.0
- New **Macro** tab: record your clicks, play them back, export (clipboard + file) and import (clipboard or saved file).
- **Click extrapolation** (Macro tab, page 2): saves recorded clicks a few ticks earlier to make up for input delay.
- Playback counts as a cheat, so Safe mode limits it to Practice and Test mode.
- HUD shows REC / PLAY while a macro is active.

## v1.1.0
- Safe mode button (two taps to turn it off) always visible at the bottom of the menu.
- English, Spanish and Russian (language button). Own font with Latin accents and Cyrillic.
- The ball can be dragged and its position is saved. "Reset ball" brings it back.
- The ball is hidden in levels and returns in the pause menu (can be turned off).
- Help text of each option appears under the rows instead of a popup.
