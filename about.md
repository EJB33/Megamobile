# MegaMobile
**by EJB3** - English / Espanol / Russian

A touch-friendly mod menu for Geometry Dash. Tap the floating **MM** ball to open it, drag it where you want.
The ball is hidden while you play a level and comes back in the pause menu.

- **Macro:** record and play your clicks, export/import them (clipboard or file) and use click extrapolation.
- **Player:** noclip with a minimum-accuracy rule, jump hack, slow motion, custom respawn time.
- **Visual:** no glow, no shaders, no particles, no death effect, hide player, no camera triggers, no mirror.
- **Performance:** auto Low Detail Mode and an Eco mode that turns shaders and new particles off while your FPS is low.
- **HUD:** FPS, session stats, noclip accuracy and a cheat indicator.
- **Stats:** attempts, deaths, furthest death, last deaths and your hot zone.
- **Presets:** Panic (everything off), Practice, Performance, Minimal visuals.
- **Safe mode:** a button at the bottom of the menu. ON (default): cheats only work in Practice or Test mode. Turning it OFF takes two taps.
- **Language:** a button at the bottom switches between English, Spanish and Russian.
